Excel for Data Analysis

Clean, sort and summarise data with formulas, pivot tables and charts. Four weeks to your first analyst-ready skills.

About this course

Start from a blank sheet and finish able to turn messy data into clear reports. You will practise on real sales, survey and finance datasets, and end with a dashboard you can show to employers.

No experience needed. If you can open a spreadsheet, you can start. This course leads to roles like Junior Data Analyst.

What you will learn

  • Clean and sort messy data
  • Write formulas: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, IF and SUMIFS
  • Build pivot tables to summarise thousands of rows
  • Create charts and a one-page dashboard

Course outline

Week 1

Spreadsheet basics and data cleaning

Tables, sorting, filters, removing duplicates and fixing errors.

Week 2

Formulas and functions

Lookups, logic and conditional sums on real datasets.

Week 3

Pivot tables and charts

Summarise, group and visualise your findings.

Week 4

Capstone: sales dashboard

Build and present a complete dashboard from raw data.

Questions

Do I need to buy Excel?

You need a copy of Microsoft Excel (2016 or newer) or Excel for the web, which is free with a Microsoft account.

How much time do I need each week?

Plan for about 5 to 6 hours a week, including practice tasks.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive a certificate when you finish the capstone dashboard.
