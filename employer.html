Help shape the skills learners build

Tell us what your roles need. We turn it into courses and meet you with learners who are ready to work.

Who can partner with us

Employers

Share the skills you hire for and find learners trained for those roles.

Training providers and colleges

List your courses and match them to the roles employers ask for.

Mentors and industry experts

Guide learners and review their projects.

What partners get

  • Access to learners with verified skills
  • A say in course content and skill requirements
  • A company profile, sponsored projects and hiring events

What we ask from partners

  • The skills and tools needed for your roles
  • Feedback on course content
  • Internship, project or mentoring opportunities
