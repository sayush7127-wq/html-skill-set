We start from the job, not the syllabus

Many graduates learn things employers do not ask for, and employers cannot find people with the skills they need. Skill nest closes that gap.

How we do it

Four steps take a learner from a career goal to proof of skill.

Listen to industryWe collect skills from job postings, employer input and frameworks like NSDC.
Map skills to rolesEach role gets a list of required skills and levels.
Build learning pathwaysCourses are chosen or designed to close each skill gap.
Prove itProjects and assessments give learners evidence to show employers.

What we believe

Role first

We begin with what a job needs, then work backwards to what to learn.

Practical

Every course ends with a project you can show.

Open to all

We use free resources wherever we can.

Kept current

Skill lists are updated as industry needs change.
