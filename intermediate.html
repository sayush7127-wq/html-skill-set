SQL and Dashboards

Query databases, join tables and present results in a clear dashboard. Six weeks to the core skill every data analyst uses daily.

About this course

Learn to ask databases the right questions and share the answers visually. Every week ends with a graded query challenge, and you finish by building a business report from a real database.

You should be comfortable with spreadsheets or have finished Excel for Data Analysis. This course leads to roles like Data Analyst.

What you will learn

  • Write SELECT queries with filters and sorting
  • Join and aggregate data from multiple tables
  • Use subqueries and window functions
  • Present results in a clear dashboard

Course outline

Week 1

SELECT, WHERE and ORDER BY

Read data from a table, filter rows and sort results.

Week 2

Aggregation and GROUP BY

Count, sum and average data by category.

Week 3

Joins

Combine customers, orders and products into one result.

Week 4

Subqueries and window functions

Rankings, running totals and comparisons over time.

Week 5

Dashboard design

Turn query results into charts people can act on.

Week 6

Capstone: business report

Query, analyse and present a full report from raw tables.
